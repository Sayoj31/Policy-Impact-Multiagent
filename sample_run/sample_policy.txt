Proposed Policy: Urban Congestion Pricing for City Center Zones

The municipal government proposes to introduce a congestion charge for
private motor vehicles entering the designated city-center zone on
weekdays between 8:00 AM and 8:00 PM. Vehicles would be charged a flat
fee per entry, tracked via automatic number-plate recognition cameras at
zone boundaries. Public transport vehicles, emergency services, and
registered vehicles for persons with disabilities would be exempt.
Revenue collected would be earmarked for public transit expansion,
including new bus rapid transit corridors and cycling infrastructure.
The stated objectives are to reduce traffic congestion and air
pollution in the city center, encourage a shift toward public and
non-motorized transport, and generate sustainable funding for transit
infrastructure. The policy would be piloted for 12 months with quarterly
public reporting on traffic volume, air quality, and revenue figures
before a decision is made on permanent adoption.
